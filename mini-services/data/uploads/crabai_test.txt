crabAI Customer FAQ

Q: What is crabAI?
A: crabAI is a custom RAG and SQL Agent engineering service. We build production-grade
retrieval-augmented generation systems and natural-language SQL agents for businesses.

Q: How long does a typical project take?
A: A Starter RAG MVP takes 2 weeks. A full Pro deployment (RAG + SQL) takes 4-5 weeks.
Enterprise engagements with on-prem deployment take 6-12 weeks.

Q: What's the pricing?
A: Starter: $4,500 fixed. Pro: $12,000 fixed. Enterprise: custom scope.
Annual support contracts are available.

Q: What compliance standards do you support?
A: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and FedRAMP. We can sign BAAs for regulated industries.

Q: Where is the data stored?
A: Wherever you want — your AWS, GCP, Azure, or on-prem. We never train on customer data.
